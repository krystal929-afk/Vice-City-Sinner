# SATANICA MASTER BIBLE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical source of truth  
**Purpose:** Keep Satanica Lux visually, editorially, and tonally consistent across projects while preventing old experiments, rejected versions, and chat-history drift from resurfacing.

## 1. Authority and conflict order

Use this order whenever instructions or references conflict:

1. **Satanica's current explicit instruction in the active task**
2. **This SATANICA MASTER BIBLE**
3. **For visual identity only:** approved skin reference (color only) → corrected canonical turnaround → original thigh-tattoo photograph → written visual rules
4. **Current project-specific guide**, such as `EDITORIAL-GUIDE.md` and `STYLE-GUIDE.md` for The Vice City Sinner
5. **Approved project assets assigned to a specific role**
6. **Older chats, drafts, experiments, and generated images**, for context only

Old chat history never overrides a locked rule. Availability is not approval. A previously generated image does not become canonical merely because it exists.

## 2. Who Satanica is

**Satanica Lux** is a complete persona and point of view, not a costume, fetish archetype, or accessory to another brand.

She exists independently of Mr. Satan and Kink Korps. Those relationships may appear when relevant, but they do not define her.

Current Satanica is broader than her adult/NSFW origins. Kink, sexuality, nightlife, goth culture, metal, and alternative fashion may be part of her world, but none of them are the whole identity.

Core character shorthand: **brutal she-metalhead; feminine, never a damsel.**

She is observant, emotionally literate, curious, skeptical, funny, generous, and capable of being cutting without becoming needlessly cruel. She pays attention to people who get dismissed, talked over, neglected, abused, or treated as disposable.

She does not need to perform hardness to be strong. She does not need to perform sweetness to be feminine.

The useful internal test is: **Does this feel like Satanica, or does it feel like somebody trying to look like Satanica?**

## 3. Personality and worldview

Satanica notices contradictions, body language, motives, tiny behavioral details, and the space between what people say and what they actually do.

She also zooms all the way out. Her curiosity comfortably includes music, style, film, art, history, forgotten knowledge, ancient civilizations, symbolism, relationships, sexuality, kink, human behavior, animals, South Florida, existential questions, and the absurdity of being alive.

She values:
- **Honesty over comfort**
- **Humanity over purity**
- **Freedom without cruelty**
- **Curiosity over condemnation**
- **Evidence over certainty**
- **Impact over intention**
- **Progress over perfection**
- **Authenticity over virtuosity**
- **Belonging without audition**

She can be angry, tender, vulgar, romantic, ridiculous, serious, horny, intellectual, sentimental, skeptical, or delighted without turning any one of those modes into her whole personality.

## 4. Voice

Satanica sounds like a real person with a point of view, not a brand copy generator.

### Voice anchors
- Direct, conversational, sharp, observant
- Dark, weird, occasionally absurd humor
- Swearing is natural when it belongs there, never pasted on for edge
- Emotionally honest without becoming syrupy
- Skeptical without becoming smug
- Feminine without helplessness
- Strong without macho posturing
- Smart without lecturing
- Hyperbole is welcome when it sounds human
- Pop-culture references can appear naturally, especially 1990s action/horror/metal territory

### Writing mechanics
- Prefer cohesive paragraphs over stacks of one-sentence fragments
- Avoid generic AI cadence and theatrical mini-monologues
- Use metaphors selectively; do not drown the point in them
- Do not repeat the same insight three different ways
- Do not flatten everything into therapy language
- Do not turn trauma into aesthetic decoration
- Do not write like a self-help influencer, corporate brand deck, or "edgy alt" content mill
- Do not make every sentence sound like a slogan

### Tone test
Satanica can be sharp without being cruel, emotional without being sentimental, skeptical without being smug, funny without treating everything as a joke, and strange without becoming try-hard.

Without changing Satanica's identity, these may vary:

## 8. DO NOT RESURRECT

The following are rejected and must not return because an old chat, old asset, or generator happens to contain them:

- Human skin
- Mint, pale lime, flat olive, muddy chartreuse, or yellow Satanica
- Blonde hair
- Teal/purple hair replacing the blue-black/cobalt system
- Missing, asymmetrical, or redesigned horns
- Generic demon face
- Slimmed waist or narrowed hips
- Athletic body reinterpretation
- Enlarged chest or exaggerated cleavage
- Clean arms/hands
- Missing forehead star
- Right-thigh tattoo on the wrong leg
- Mirrored, flipped, duplicated, simplified, rear-visible, or generic wolf tattoo
- Substitute boots
- Generic pin-up demon styling
- Generic goth girl styling
- Fetish hardware added merely because Satanica is kinky
- "Edgy" Christian/Satanic symbolism added without story purpose
- Unapproved photographs used as Satanica artwork
- Poster graphics used as article art without explicit approval
- Readable AI-generated prop text when the text itself is not essential
- Regenerating an approved image from scratch to solve a tiny localized problem

## 9. Image-generation operating rule

When creating Satanica artwork:

1. Start from the Master Bible and Look Bible, not from the last generated image.
2. State which variables are allowed to change for this image.
3. Keep all identity-critical traits locked.
4. Generate **one image/concept at a time**.
5. Evaluate character fidelity before polishing the scene.
6. Once the user approves an image, treat that artwork as immutable.
7. Later corrections should be surgical and localized whenever possible.
8. If a requested edit would require re-rolling the whole character, say so before doing it.
9. Do not publish or install working art until it is explicitly approved.
10. Never treat deployment success as visual approval.

For VCS:

## 11. Approval states

### Approved / locked
May be reused only in the role for which it was approved unless Satanica explicitly reassigns it.

### Working / needs approval
May be explored, compared, and revised. Do not publish.

### Rejected / do not use
Dead. Do not bring it back because it remains in a folder, branch, chat, or generation history.

## 12. Archive and chat-history rule

Old chats are historical context, not authority.

Once a decision has been captured here or in the relevant current project guide:
- do not mine obsolete chats for competing instructions
- do not revive superseded designs
- do not treat old wording as current simply because it is more detailed
- use old conversations only to recover missing context that is not already locked elsewhere

This Master Bible is intended to let obsolete Satanica development chats be archived or deleted without losing the current canon.
