SARITA BON BON TAKES THE ROPE

At midnight, the Anything Goes floor becomes a stage for shibari by Sarita Bon Bon with rope bunny Sweet Thickness.

THE VICE CITY SINNER · AUGUST 2026

Anything Goes adds a live centerpiece to its August return: a midnight shibari performance led by Sarita Bon Bon, joined by rope bunny Sweet Thickness.

For first-time guests, a performance offers a useful way to experience rope as more than an image. Watch the pace, communication, body positioning, transitions, and attention between the people involved. The visible shape is only one part of the work.

Watch the connection, not only the knots.

SEE THE PERFORMANCE

Friday, August 28 · Midnight
Nu Vibez Lounge · 1745 Northwest 38th Avenue, Lauderhill, Florida

Anything Goes begins at 10 PM and continues until 4 AM. Admission is 21+. Advance tickets are $15 and door admission is $20. Free parking is available.

Performance etiquette: Give performers and equipment room. Do not touch rope, bodies, or gear. Follow the venue's photography policy. Save questions for an appropriate moment rather than interrupting active play.
