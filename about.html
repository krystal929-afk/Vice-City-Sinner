About Satanica

I’m Satanica Lux, creator and editor of Vice City Sinner. I’m a heavy metal girl, lifelong observer of people, and someone who has always been more interested in what exists outside the center of the room. Music, nightlife, sexuality, identity, art, relationships, history, style, and the stranger corners of human behavior have a lot more in common than people give them credit for.

I created Vice City Sinner intentionally: to build the kind of publication I wanted to read. Something independent, curious, intelligent without being pretentious, and willing to give real attention to subjects that are often reduced to trends, stereotypes, or clickbait. South Florida is home base, but the conversation is much bigger than South Florida, and much bigger than any single scene.

I’m not interested in telling people what they’re supposed to like or think. I want to tell good stories, find the interesting part everyone else walked past, and occasionally ask a question that ruins a perfectly good afternoon because now you need to know the answer too.
